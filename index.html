About

Hey! I am a programmer and researcher. Other than that, I like the outdoors, hiking, skiing, sailing. I also like reading. My most fun fact is that one time I walked from Massachusets to Canada. I plan to write about this and many more of my interests here, but for now I'm starting with research.

I studied cs/math/physics at Northeastern as an undergrad. Research wise, I am broadly interested in quantum information theory, machine learning, and algorithms. Previously I've thought about magic state distillation, random purification, and code editing models. Currently, I would like to find more applications of the Efficient Hermite Transform. In terms of industry experience, I've worked both in finance and traditional tech.
